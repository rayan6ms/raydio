# Playback audit repairs — 2026-10-03

All seven findings from [the audit](BUG-AUDIT-2026-10-03.md) now have code fixes
and regression coverage. The original characterization evidence describes the
baseline; its tests deliberately asserted faults and are not the release gate.

| Finding | Corrected behavior | Regression evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 — End watchdog | Correlates actual progress with the Play generation; unchanged updates cannot conceal a stall. An expired metadata deadline triggers verification, not automatic completion. Natural advancement requires a matching finished terminal event; a confirmed 60-second stall follows failure semantics. Verification failure has five-second backoff and a 90-second no-progress bound. Pause/reconnect/EOF drain keep their separate lifecycle behavior. | Delayed progress, stale generations, unchanged position, active expiry, confirmed/missing terminal events, repeated terminal observations, bounded unverifiable recovery, pause. |
| B2 — Unknown duration | Zero remains the existing **finite unknown** wire sentinel. Numeric/string durations parse; absent/malformed duration stays unknown. No duration cutoff or near-end preparation is inferred. Views show real elapsed time and identify missing duration. | Source parser variants; healthy finite song beyond 15 seconds; unknown preparation and display; known-duration and stream policies. |
| B3 — Unplayable entries | Requires boolean `isPlayable:true`. False/null/absent/invalid entries never consume playback queue slots; surrounding valid entries retain order. | Mixed unavailable renderers with a two-track capacity and ordered valid results. |
| B4 — Cold opening limits | Actual cold Play acquires shared load/source/outbound capacity in the adapter; warm adoption and completed replay remain offline. Identifier discovery releases its finished permits before opening. A replacement cannot reuse an old source's live connection lease. | Saturated HTTP cold Play/retry; real actor cold/warm/cache paths; replacement, cancellation/error and abandoned-reader lease recovery. |
| B5 — Cleanup ACKs | Player-destroying cleanup aborts **and drains** preparation results. Same-player Stop still remembers overtaken accepted intent and submits its cancellation in order. | Already-completed and in-flight ACK cleanup, no idle retries, Stop ordering, rejoin with the same successor. |
| B6 — Shared cancellation | Separate single-owner OAuth/script gates wait cancellably and with a deadline, checking every 10 ms. State locks are short and are not held over HTTP. No waiter list or new dependency. | Real HTTP owner/waiter contention, cancelled waiter returns within a 150 ms test budget while owner remains pending, survivor shares cache, failure/retry, timeout and shutdown. |
| B7 — Previous capacity | Refuses a full queue transactionally and explains the refusal. Queue-loop deduplication may free its own slot; no unrelated requested track is discarded. | Off/Track/Queue, repeated refusal, intact history/current/upcoming, successful restoration and queue-repeat deduplication. |

An additional admission-lifetime correction keeps a prepared source's connection
permit after adoption and through detached blocking read/control disposal. The
actor's session and lease result drops media before releasing admission. This
closes a release-at-adoption hole encountered while implementing B4.

Crust GET and playerUpdate expose a small `crust` observation with current
`userData` and the last terminal event's `userData`/reason. That terminal observation
is retained before critical event publication, enabling verified recovery after
missed delivery. Preparation's requested/opened/ready/consumed/failure summaries
are enabled in Raydio's normal log filter; no descriptors or source URLs are logged.
